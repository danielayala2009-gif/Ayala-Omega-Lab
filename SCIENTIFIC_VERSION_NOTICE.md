## Versioning rule

Changes to the benchmark battery, seed logic, scoring rules, comparators,
null-calibration definition, or audit-ledger semantics constitute a
**new scientific version**.

UI, icon, packaging, installation, caching, and platform-wrapper changes
may be released as packaging revisions only when they leave the frozen
scientific core unchanged.

Modified forks must identify themselves as modified and must not imply
that altered results were generated by the unchanged official frozen
benchmark.
